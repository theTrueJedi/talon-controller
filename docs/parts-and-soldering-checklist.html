1 · Parts hunt: find it, check it, photograph both sides

PartCheckNotes / label text
Waveshare ESP32-C3-Zero (any 1 of 5)Bare holes, no pins. Count the GND holes; photo the pin labels. A header strip to cut pins from
12-LED ring (any 1 of 5)Back pad labels: DI/DIN 5V/VCC GND DO/DOUT, plus any model text
KY-040 knob5 pins attached: CLK DT SW + GND. Pressing the shaft clicks
Jumper wires, female–femaleSocket on both ends. Needed for the knob (5 wires)
HY2.0 → 2.54 mm cablesSmall 4-pin plug on one end, separate sockets on the other
USB-C cableMust carry data, not charge-only. We'll confirm when testing
Why the HY2.0 cables matter: cut off the 4-pin plug and solder 3 loose ends to the ring. The socket ends then push straight onto the board's pins, giving you a plug-in ring from parts you own. No F–F jumpers? A pack costs a few dollars and is likely the only purchase.

2 · Soldering kit check (ANBES 60 W)

  • Iron, stand, tips (fine conical or small chisel)
  • Solder wire: thin rosin-core, 0.6–0.8 mm, 63/37 or lead-free (may not be in the kit)
  • Flux pen or paste: makes small pads much easier
  • Wire strippers or a sharp blade
  • Tape or a clamp to hold the ring
  • Optional: multimeter, to check for shorts between pads

Shopping list (if missing)

  • F–F jumper wires
  • Solder wire
  • Flux
  • Data USB-C cable

3 · Soldering setup & practice

  • Iron about 330 °C (leaded) or 360 °C (lead-free)
  • Ventilation (open window) and glasses
  • Practice on a spare ring (you have 5). For each joint:
  1. Put a little solder on the pad.
  2. Put a little solder on the stripped wire end.
  3. Touch them together with the iron for about 2 s.
  • Board header pins, only the holes in use: 5V GND 3V3 GPIO4–7. Push the short ends through from the bottom, stand the board in a breadboard to keep them straight, then solder from the top. Practice on a spare C3-Zero first
  • 3 joints on the real ring: 5V GND DIN (the input side)
